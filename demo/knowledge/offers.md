# Current offers

## Winter flu vaccination week (1–15 December 2026)
Walk-in flu vaccinations every weekday from 09:00 to 11:00, no appointment needed.
Price: 30 EUR including the vaccine (normally 48 EUR). Free for patients over 60 and for pregnant patients.

## Family check-up package
Book health check-ups for two adults and a child check-up in the same week and pay 210 EUR instead of 240 EUR.
Mention "family package" when booking. Valid until 31 January 2027.

## Students
Students with a valid student ID get 10% off general and extended consultations for self-paying patients.
