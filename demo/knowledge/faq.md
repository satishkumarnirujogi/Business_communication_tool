# Frequently asked questions

## Where are you and how do I get there?
Riverside Family Clinic, 12 River Road, Springfield. Bus lines 4 and 17 stop at "River Road" directly outside.

## Is there parking?
Yes. Our car park behind the building has 20 spaces, free for patients for up to 2 hours. Two spaces are
reserved for disabled drivers next to the entrance.

## Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?
Yes. There is step-free access, a lift to the first floor and an accessible toilet.

## What should I bring to my first visit?
Your insurance card, a photo ID, a list of the medicines you take and any recent test results or doctor's letters.

## Can I get a repeat prescription without an appointment?
Repeat prescriptions for regular medication can be requested at the front desk or by phone and are ready
for collection after two working days. New medication always needs an appointment.

## Do you offer video consultations?
Not at the moment. All appointments take place at the clinic.

## Do you see children?
Yes. Dr. Sofia Alvarez is our pediatrician. Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.
