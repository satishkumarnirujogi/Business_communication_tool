# Riverside Family Clinic – Patient Policies

## Cancellations and missed appointments
Appointments can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the start time, online or by phone.
Within 48 hours, please call the front desk on +1-555-0100. A late-cancellation fee of 25 EUR may apply.
If you miss an appointment without cancelling (no-show), we charge a fee of 40 EUR. After two no-shows
we may ask for a deposit before your next booking.

## Arriving late
Please arrive 10 minutes before your appointment. If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to
shorten or rebook your appointment so other patients are not kept waiting.

## Payment
We accept debit cards, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) and cash. Payment is due at the end of the visit.
Invoices for self-paying patients are sent by email within 3 working days and are due within 14 days.

## Insurance
We work with all statutory health insurers and most private insurers. Please bring your insurance card
to every visit. Private patients receive an invoice they can submit to their insurer.

## Privacy
Your medical records are confidential and are only shared with your consent or where the law requires it.
You can request a copy of your records at the front desk; we provide it within 30 days.

## Medical questions
The online assistant and front desk cannot give medical advice. For medical questions please book an
appointment or ask to speak to a nurse. In an emergency call 112.
